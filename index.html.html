Key Findings & Insights
🏆
Extreme Greed = Highest Avg PnL
Surprisingly, Extreme Greed produced the highest avg PnL per trade ($130.21) and the highest win rate (89%). Traders here are predominantly short-biased — successfully fading the euphoria. The $166/trade short avg PnL during Extreme Greed is the single best signal in the dataset.
📉
Fear Regime = Best Shorting Alpha
During regular Fear periods, short positions generate a remarkable $153.54 avg PnL — second only to Extreme Greed shorts. This is counter-intuitive: fear doesn't always mean the bottom. Traders profitably shorting further drawdowns during fear is a key hidden pattern.
⚠️
Greed Regime — Volume Trap
Greed sees a massive volume spike ($288M+) but has the second-lowest avg PnL ($85.40) and win rate (77%). The only liquidation also occurred here. Traders over-trade during greed, diluting returns and increasing risk — a classic FOMO trap.
⏰
The 12:00–13:00 IST Edge
Trades executed at 12:00–13:00 IST produce $243 avg PnL — nearly 3× the dataset average. This coincides with European market open and Asian close. A time-filtered strategy restricting entries to 10:00–13:00 IST could meaningfully boost risk-adjusted returns.
🧠
Sentiment Correlation is Weak — But Regime Matters
The raw correlation between daily fear/greed value and avg PnL is only +0.0013 (near zero). However, regime classification (the 5-bucket label) reveals significant behavioral differences. The index is more useful as a behavioral classifier than a price predictor.
💎
Fear Drives Volume, Greed Drives Returns
Fear periods attract the most capital ($483M volume) and generate the most total PnL ($3.36M), but Extreme Greed has the highest per-trade efficiency. This suggests a two-phase strategy: accumulate size in fear, optimize per-trade quality in greed.

Methodology & Data Notes
Data Processing
Trader timestamps (IST format: DD-MM-YYYY HH:MM) were parsed and normalized to date for joining with the Fear/Greed index. Match rate: 99.997%. Closed PnL analysis restricted to non-zero PnL trades (104,408 of 211,224) representing actual position-close events.
Limitations
Only 32 unique traders — results may not generalize. Fear/Greed Index is a lagging sentiment indicator. Short-alpha findings may reflect survivorship bias among sophisticated Hyperliquid users. Leverage data was not used in this analysis and could further refine risk-adjusted metrics.
